# Privacy Policy for MAXREP

Last updated: [Insert Today’s Date]

MAXREP (“the App”) is a school weightlifting and performance-tracking application designed for students and athletes to follow coach-created programs, log workouts, and monitor progress. This Privacy Policy explains how MAXREP collects, uses, and protects user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
- Email address  
- Password (securely stored and encrypted)

### 1.2 Workout & Performance Data
- Exercises completed  
- Sets, reps, weight, and progress  
- Coach-assigned programs and training logs  

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information
We may collect basic technical information such as:
- Device type  
- General usage data  
We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or unnecessary personal data.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to:
- Create and maintain accounts  
- Log workouts and track progress  
- Allow coaches to assign and monitor programs  
- Improve app features and performance  
We do not sell user data or share it with advertisers.

## 3. Data Security
We use modern security practices including:
- Encrypted password storage  
- Secure database access controls  
No method is 100% secure, but we use industry-standard protections.

## 4. Information Sharing
We may share information only with:
- School coaches or administrators  
- Secure database hosting services  
- Legal authorities if required  
We never sell your data.

## 5. Students and Minors
MAXREP may be used by minors in school environments:
- Accounts must be created under school/coach approval  
- Data is used strictly for educational/athletic purposes  
- Complies with applicable student data privacy laws
